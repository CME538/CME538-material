Lecture 5.5 data sources

All notebook examples read local files. Historical snapshots describe their recorded periods.

Added local datasets

  • spy_daily_close_2010_2020.csv: 2,769 trading-day closes for SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY), 4 January 2010 to 31 December 2020. Retrieved through yfinance from Yahoo Finance historical data on 5 October 2026. Download settings: start='2010-01-01', end='2021-01-01', auto_adjust=False. Only the Date and Close fields are retained. Close is the provider's unadjusted-close field in USD per share, rather than adjusted close or index points. The notebook retains the original price and first-difference demonstrations using this fixed classroom extract.
  • illustrative_reservoir_daily.csv: constructed CME538 teaching data, 180 daily rows starting 1 January 2024. Rainfall is generated from intermittent gamma-distributed amounts. Water level combines a gradual rise, a seven-day operating cycle, a short rainfall response, and random variation. Seed: 538. Seven water-level observations are deliberately missing. These values are neither recorded measurements nor a calibrated reservoir model. Fields: date, rainfall_mm (daily depth), water_level_m (a daily level reading).

Existing course files

  • bike_traffic_data.csv: City of Seattle Fremont Bridge bicycle counter. The course extract runs from October 2012 to July 2019 and records hourly crossings on two sidewalks. Counter totals are crossings rather than unique people or journey purposes.
  • city_temperatures.csv: Melbourne monthly temperature series attributed in the course materials to Berkeley Earth. The supplied extract contains 1841–2013 records and uncertainty estimates. Exact export history is absent from the course bundle. The additions use complete months from 1990–2012 without filling gaps.
  • covid19_canada.csv: historical Government of Canada COVID-19 surveillance extract. The displayed national series uses the file's supplied seven-day average.
  • Annual_Canadian_Lynx_trappings.csv: annual Canadian lynx trappings, 1821–1934. See the R datasets documentation and source references. Trapping counts differ from a direct census of the animal population.
  • earthquakes.csv, brick_production.csv, monthly_red_meat_slaughter.csv, and stacked_overflow.csv: pre-existing course extracts retained unchanged. Exact original download records and redistribution licences are absent from the bundle. The notebook identifies the series and supplied units without asserting new provenance.
