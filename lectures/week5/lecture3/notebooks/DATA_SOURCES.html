Lecture 5.3 data sources

Restaurant tips

tips.csv is the existing course copy of the Seaborn tips dataset. Each of the 244 source rows records a restaurant bill and its tip. The notebooks retain the first 243 rows to match the established slide examples. No recorded values are changed. The calculated field tip_pct is 100 times tip divided by total_bill. Bill and tip amounts are in USD. The data are an observational sample from a restaurant setting, not a representative sample of all customers.

The copies beside Lectures 5.3 and 5.4 are byte-identical to the existing Lecture 5.2 CSV. Files are loaded locally during class.

Constructed sensor calibration readings

illustrative_sensor_calibration.csv contains 96 constructed conditions. Fields: reference_kpa (reference pressure in kPa), temperature_c (temperature in degrees Celsius), sensor_kpa (sensor reading in kPa). The identical file appears in Lectures 5.3 and 5.4 so the same example continues across lessons.

Generation uses NumPy default_rng(538). Reference pressure is uniform from 20 to 220 kPa. Temperature is uniform from 5 to 35 degrees Celsius. The response is 8 + 1.06 * reference_kpa + 0.35 * (temperature_c - 20) + noise, with independent normal noise of standard deviation 1.5 kPa. Saved values are rounded to four decimals. These are original CME538 teaching values, not field measurements or an instrument calibration standard.

The fitted models describe sensor response to known reference pressure. They do not perform inverse calibration. Loss comparisons use the fitted rows and do not estimate future prediction performance. In the constructed data, temperature is an omitted input in the pressure-only model.
