Lecture 5.2 data sources

Restaurant tips

tips.csv is the existing course copy of the Seaborn tips dataset. Each of the 244 source rows records a restaurant bill and its tip. The notebooks retain the first 243 rows to match the established slide examples. No recorded values are changed. The calculated field tip_pct is 100 times tip divided by total_bill. Bill and tip amounts are in USD. The data are an observational sample from a restaurant setting, not a representative sample of all customers.

The copies beside Lectures 5.3 and 5.4 are byte-identical to the existing Lecture 5.2 CSV. Files are loaded locally during class.

Constructed repair durations

illustrative_repair_durations.csv contains 12 invented jobs with fields job_id and duration_min. The durations are 18, 22, 24, 25, 26, 28, 30, 32, 35, 40, 45 and 120 minutes. These are original CME538 teaching values, not municipal records. The three-to-one underestimate cost is an illustrative decision assumption rather than an estimated operational cost.

Other illustrative values

The small arrays in the loss-shape examples and the additional tip percentages in the sensitivity example are explicitly constructed. They leave the restaurant CSV unchanged.

Software documentation

Seaborn histogram and density plots, NumPy least squares, Jupyter slider controls.

Environment

The course environment supplies NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn. Lectures 5.3 and 5.4 also use ipywidgets. Notebooks do not install packages or request network data. Static figures accompany interactive views so saved results remain readable without a widget frontend.
