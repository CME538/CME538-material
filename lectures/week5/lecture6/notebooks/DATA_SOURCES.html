Lecture 5.6 data sources

Toronto Public Library branches

toronto_library_branches_2023.csv contains 100 physical branches from the Toronto Public Library Library Branch General Information 2023 CSV. Retrieved on 5 October 2026 from the City of Toronto Open Data portal. The 112-row source also contains 12 other services. This extract selects PhysicalBranch == 1 and retains BranchCode, BranchName, Address, Lat and Long. No coordinates are invented or geocoded.

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto. Source: Toronto Public Library / City of Toronto.

Original CSV resource.

Existing Toronto layers

stations.csv contains 608 Bike Share Toronto locations in the existing course snapshot. The source retrieval date is absent from the bundle. toronto_neighbourhoods.shp and its companion files contain the older 140-neighbourhood boundary system with WGS84 coordinates. Preserve the .shp, .shx, .dbf, .prj and .cpg files together.

Related publisher pages: Bike Share Toronto and Toronto neighbourhoods. The notebook retains the supplied historical files. The examples do not measure current station availability, service quality, population access or travel time.

Illustrative geometry

The three-shape example uses invented local metre coordinates for a point, line and polygon. No real site, road or property boundary is represented. The existing UofT and Toronto City Centre weather-station points are retained as supplied by the course.

Measurement and comparison

EPSG:4326 stores longitude and latitude in degrees. The distance demonstrations transform the Toronto layers to WGS84 / UTM zone 17N (EPSG:32617). Distances are planar straight-line distances in metres. Circular buffers exclude street networks, entrances, barriers and travel time. Combining a 2023 library extract with an undated station extract demonstrates methods, rather than a same-date service audit.
