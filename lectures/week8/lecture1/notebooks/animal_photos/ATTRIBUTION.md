# Animal photograph attribution

The cat and dog photographs are unchanged 480-pixel teaching copies from the Week 7.2 materials, originally downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-08-30. The rabbit photographs were downloaded at their original resolution on 2026-10-24 and are stored unchanged. Figures may crop and resize photographs for display. Follow each source link for the original image, creator details and full licence terms.

These are teaching examples with assigned class predictions. The photographs were not used to train an image classifier.
